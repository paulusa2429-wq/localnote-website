Use case · Consulting

Scope agreed on the call. Scope drifted in the statement of work.

Consultants sell clarity, and engagements leak margin and trust in the gap between the discovery call and the written document. Proofaro records what was actually agreed — on your own computer, with no bot on the call — and checks the statement of work, the change request and the status report against those words, with the original quote as evidence.

Where engagements quietly change

Listen to the call again in your head. The client’s problem is urgent and the scope comes together out loud: six workshops, a board readout, the data migration included, a first draft by the 15th. You price it in the room, the client says yes, and everyone leaves believing the same engagement exists. Then the statement of work arrives from procurement — or, on a small engagement, from your own template — and it describes a slightly different job: four workshops, the migration moved to “phase two,” the draft due at month-end. Each change is small enough to wave through. Together they re-price the engagement, stretch the timeline, and move work you priced as included into a column nobody agreed to pay for.

And if you sign, the document wins every later argument. Three months on, when the client asks where the other two workshops went, “but we agreed on the call” is a memory, and memories negotiate badly against paper. The drift is rarely malice — it is templates, handoffs and assumptions — but the consultant carries the cost either way: unpaid scope, a stretched calendar, and a relationship spent on archaeology instead of the work.

What Proofaro does for consultants

The call, recorded without a bot. Clients speak candidly about budgets, politics and problems when no recording bot is sitting in the participant list. Proofaro records on your own computer; the recording and transcript never leave it, and transcription runs on-device — fully offline after a one-time setup download, which matters when your office is a client site with no guest Wi-Fi worth trusting.

Commitments with receipts. Every promise — yours and the client’s — lands in the Commitments Ledger with its date and its original wording. Who agreed to send the data by Thursday? Who moved the readout? It stops being recollection and becomes a record you can check in seconds, across every engagement you run.

Answers you can defend. When a client says “that is not what we agreed,” you can answer with the moment itself — and, if it ever turns serious, hand over a self-verifying evidence pack: an export whose fingerprints let the other side confirm nothing was altered after export, built from your own record rather than your memory of it.

Documents checked on arrival. Bring Proofaro the SOW, the change request or the client’s marked-up summary, and Said vs Written compares it against the calls: deliverable counts, dates, and what was included versus moved or billed separately — each difference flagged with the original quote. A response draft is prepared for your review; nothing is sent on its own. See Said vs Written and commitment tracking for the mechanics.

It remembers across engagements, too. Ask what was agreed with this client in March — the rate, the review cadence, the thing the sponsor cared about — and the answer is built from your transcripts, with the original quote attached and anything the record cannot confirm marked as unconfirmed. When a client returns a year later, the relationship picks up from the record, not from your recollection of it.

Built for one-person and small firms

There is no admin console to configure and no client data sitting on a vendor’s server to explain in a procurement review — a sentence you can say plainly to a careful client: the recordings live on my computer, and nowhere else. Your mornings start ahead, too: today’s brief is ready, yesterday’s documents have been checked against yesterday’s words, and the drafts are waiting in your Morning Queue. Proofaro doesn’t just remember what happened. It helps catch what could go wrong next.

Questions people ask

Will my clients know they are recorded?

There is no bot and no extra participant — Proofaro records on your computer. The app shows you a consent reminder as recording starts, but telling your clients and getting their consent is your responsibility, as it should be.

What documents can it check?

The ones that carry the engagement: statements of work, proposals, change requests and written summaries. Bring the document to Proofaro and it is compared against the recorded words — deliverables, dates and inclusions — with the original quote shown for every flag.

Can I prove what was agreed months later?

Yes. The recording, transcript and ledger stay on your computer, and an exported evidence pack carries a fingerprint of every file so the other side can verify nothing changed after export.
