SUNDAY TO FRIDAY · ONE CONNECTED WEEK

One week. One thread.

This is one week in one consultant's work — Sunday night to Friday evening — and one client: the Acme renewal, the same fictional sample meeting built into LocalNote, with Maya Chen and Dana Park of Acme. Nothing here resets. The note he speaks on Sunday is pinned to his screen on Monday; the brief built on Monday is practised, then carried into the room on Tuesday; the promises made on Tuesday are chased on Wednesday, disputed on Thursday, and landed on Friday. Follow the thread and you follow the whole product — every feature below is at work inside the story, because that is the only honest way to show it.

Every name, number and line on this page is fictional — it is the app's own sample data, shown so you can see the work, not just the claims. Nothing here is a real customer or a real call.

The week: Sunday night · Monday · Tuesday · Wednesday · Thursday · Friday · Friday evening

THURSDAY · WHAT THE RECORD SUPPORTS

It writes a finding — the two readings, and the rule that compared them — and waits for his decision.

Every claim in the case either stands on a citation or is marked as standing on nothing. That is the whole method — and it is why the packet he is about to send can be checked by anyone who receives it.

THURSDAY · THE PACKET LEAVES

The case, exported — and a key that opens only this case.

Before anything leaves, the Privacy Vault does its work: the export is checked against the consent log — Tuesday's consent, the late joiner's re-consent, all of it in the encrypted, hash-chained log — and his redaction rule is applied, the way it is applied to every export by default. Then he exports the case for Acme's finance team. The preview comes first, and the preview is the file — the exact bytes that will be written, with the redaction already applied and its count on the preview's face. The approval is bound to the packet's digest: if the case changes after the preview, the export refuses and asks for a fresh look, so what he approved is what leaves. He taps Export. Two files land in the folder he chose — the packet, and a manifest that lets the packet be re-verified later. There is no send button. He sends it himself, from his own mail, the way everything leaves.

That is the same machinery as the audit pack, and it gets used the same afternoon. His AI assistant app asks what the case concluded. The assistant already holds a broad permission — all his meetings — and under it, it can see no case at all: cases are never inside “all”. Cases have their own door. He grants the cases permission for this one case, read-only — and only then can the assistant list it, and read its claims, its chronology and its standings. Never the exhibits themselves: names, hashes and standings are the whole surface. Every call, and every refusal, is written to the audit log.

FRIDAY · THE PROPOSAL, CHECKED

Checked against his confirmed records — before it goes out.

The promise needs a proposal, and the proposal is a .docx that carries the week's promises in writing. Before it goes anywhere, he runs FinalCheck — the third of Monday's switches. He selects the records the draft must answer to, and they are the artefacts this story has been stacking up: the case findings from Thursday, the ledger, the signed SOW. Nothing is pre-selected for him; the records he confirms are frozen as the authority, hashes and all, and the draft is checked against exactly those.

FinalCheck · Findings (fictional)

Where a single exact replacement exists, a minimal repair is proposed — the confirmed words substituted for the draft's, never a recomputed figure, never a rewritten sentence. He approves each repair on its own; every approval is bound to the hash of the draft and the hashes of the records, so if either changes, the approval goes stale instead of going through. And because the draft is a .docx, the repairs land as a repaired copy — the original file is never edited. Read-back then checks the copy, and reports in its three words: verified — the approved words are there, and nothing else moved.

The check's report lists what was examined, what was found and what he decided. One word never appears in it: it does not call the proposal correct. Correct is a claim about the world; the report only claims what the records support. He sends the proposal himself.
