The no-invention check · Free

Roast my meeting summary.

AI notetakers sound certain — even when they're inventing. Paste the summary yours gave you — from Granola, Otter, Fireflies, ChatGPT, any of them — together with the transcript of the same meeting. This page checks every claim in the summary against what was actually said, and stamps the ones the meeting never backed.

Your text never leaves this browser. There is no server behind this page — the check runs entirely on your device, and nothing you paste is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere. Don't take our word for it: open your browser's network tab, then run the check and watch — no request carries your text.

The example is a fictional meeting with one invented deadline, one invented person and two honest claims — see which stamps land where.

What this check can — and can't — do

It's a text check, not mind-reading

It compares words, not meaning. A loose paraphrase can be stamped UNCONFIRMED even when the meaning is right, if it shares too few words with anything actually said. And a clever invention can slip through, if it reuses enough of the meeting's real words.

A taste of the real thing

This page splits your summary into sentences and matches words — the deterministic subset of LocalNote's No-Invention Lint. The app runs the same check on structured facts — who promised, what, by when — over the full record: the audio, the speakers and every meeting you have. And it stamps what's unproven where everyone can see it, instead of quietly dropping it.

In LocalNote, every meeting gets this check — automatically.
