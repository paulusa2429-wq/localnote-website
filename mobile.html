LocalNote for iPhone — in development

LocalNote is a meeting assistant that understands what was said — and what needs to happen next. It lives in your pocket, and it never sends your life to a cloud. There is no new interface to learn: you just talk to it. It can record a conversation wherever it happens, transcribed on the phone itself, tell you who promised what — and show you where they said it, or nudge you before a promise slips.

LocalNote for iPhone runs its AI models on the phone itself. No cloud, no meeting bot joining your calls, no account. It is the same LocalNote as the Mac app — one product, launching together — with the same promise: your conversations stay on your device. And the two keep each other up to date — directly, over your own Wi-Fi, never through a cloud.
