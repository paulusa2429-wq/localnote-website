USE CASES

Built for people whose days are full of meetings.

LocalNote is for people whose work is promises — to clients, to a school, to a team. The professions below are examples, not niches: what they share is that a meeting makes a commitment they have to keep, price, or prove.

PEOPLE WHOSE WORK IS PROMISES

Promises you have to keep — and be able to prove.

Some of these conversations are also confidential by law, by ethics, or by safety — for those, a cloud note-taker is a risk as well as a tool. But the deeper job is the same in every entry below: what was promised stays findable, checkable, and done.
