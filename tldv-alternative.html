tl;dv is a favourite of sales teams for good reason: clips, coaching and CRM fields that fill themselves. Proofaro is built for a different job: no bot in your meetings, transcription on your own computer that works fully offline after a one-time setup download, a recording you keep, and documents checked against what was actually said.

Meet tl;dv

tl;dv calls itself an AI meeting intelligence platform for teams on Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. A tl;dv bot can join and record your calls — video included — or its Chrome extension can bring the bot to Google Meet, or its desktop app can capture audio directly with no bot in the call. Its strengths are everything that happens after: timestamped clips and reels, speaker insights and playbook coaching for sales teams, AI that reads hundreds of meetings at once, and notes that sync themselves into HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack and Notion, with 5,000+ more apps through Zapier. A free plan covers unlimited recordings and transcription, with limits on the AI extras.

One honest credit first: tl;dv hosts data in the EU, which matters to many teams. But it is still a cloud — recordings are uploaded to your tl;dv library and processed there, and free-plan recordings are kept for three months. Proofaro removes the region question entirely: the record never leaves your computer.
