Quickstart

  1. Join the waitlist on the download page - when the first public release is ready, we will email you the download link.
  2. Install the application and follow operating-system security prompts.
  3. Download the recommended on-device AI models during first-run setup.
  4. Try the built-in fictional sample meeting before recording your own.
  5. Grant recording permissions only when you are ready to capture a real meeting.

Local models

LocalNote uses on-device transcription and AI models. Downloads require internet access and additional disk space. As a guide, the current models total about 4.8 GB to download, and a computer with 8 GB of memory runs the light profile.

Do not assume the installer download includes all models. Refer to the application's first-run size indicator before installing.

Recording and consent

Recording is user-controlled. Bot-free does not mean permission-free: obtain consent when required by applicable law and your organization's policies.

Meeting follow-through

LocalNote can surface changed decisions, draft email follow-ups and prepare supported local deliverables. Review and approve outputs before sending or updating important documents.

Offline behavior

After downloading the necessary local models, core processing is designed to work without outside network services. Downloads and optional connected applications are separate online interactions.

Connected AI tools and MCP

LocalNote's MCP design uses client approval, selected-meeting scopes and read-only access. A connected hosted AI product may send retrieved text to its servers. Do not grant access to a client unless you trust how it handles content.

Exact installation and configuration commands must come from the released application's tested documentation. No unverified localhost endpoint is recommended here.

Support and troubleshooting

If recording or model setup fails, confirm system permissions, free disk space, and the chosen model profile. For known bugs and release issues, contact us by email.
