FOR WHOEVER SIGNS OFF

The control questions, answered.

Buyers who sign off ask control questions, not feature questions. The five that matter, in Risk / Control / Evidence form:

Audit trail

Risk: “If this touches client work, what did it do, and when?”

Control: every outbound connection the app makes is logged on the machine, by day, host and category; the audit pack is exportable.

Evidence: “Don’t take our word for it — the connection log is yours, on your computer, and you can hand it to your own auditor.”

Verification receipts

Risk: “Tools say ‘done’. How do I know it happened?”

Control: an approved change is written, then read back; the receipt says verified, failed, or uncertain — never “done” on the write’s own say-so, and a person’s attestation is labelled as attestation, kept apart from verified evidence.

Evidence: the completion ledger, per action.

Data residency

Risk: “Where do our client conversations end up?”

Control: nowhere — processing happens on the machine the meeting happened on. For a client asking the question, that is an architectural fact, not a policy promise that can be revised next quarter.

Evidence: it runs in airplane mode; the connection log shows the silence.

No DPA, because there is no processing by us

Risk: vendor data-processing agreements, sub-processor lists, breach surface.

Control: LocalNote never receives your data, so there is no processing by us to contract over — no per-seat cloud agreement, no sub-processors, nothing of yours on our breach surface.

Evidence: the same log; here, the absence is the control.

Procurement simplicity

Risk: implementation projects, integration risk, rollout cost quietly exceeding the licence.

Control: one download and a one-time model setup (~4.8 GB); the flagship modules are off by default and switched on one at a time; there is no integration project and no services engagement to buy.

Evidence: the download page’s four steps are the whole onboarding.

Common questions

What's included in the 30-day trial?

Everything. The trial is the full LocalNote experience for 30 days - no card required to start.

What happens after 30 days?

You drop into Free mode: your whole history stays open forever and you can record up to 3 new meetings a month. The paid plan is there whenever you want unlimited meetings and the full AI — its price will be published before launch. Your data is never locked, and there are no surprises.

Does offline AI require internet access?

Initial app and model downloads require internet. Core AI workflows are designed to work offline after setup. Optional connected clients may transmit content according to their own settings.

You haven’t published a price.

Correct, and we won’t ask for a card before we do. It’s published before launch, the first 30 days are free — and the question that matters until then is the payback one: what is one caught change order worth at your rate?

Your savings numbers are unmeasured.

Agreed — which is why we publish none. The calculator on the homepage runs on your numbers alone, and when TimeBack ships, its Ledger will separate possible from verified in your own copy. Your ledger is the measurement.

Not another subscription.

Beta testers hold the founder price — the lowest LocalNote will ever offer, locked while subscribed. And if you ever stop paying, Free mode keeps your entire history readable, searchable and exportable forever. Your records are never the hostage.

Rolling this out to a team sounds like a project.

There is no rollout project: modules are off by default, each person switches on what they need, and nothing connects to anything without that person’s own decision.

Our data can’t sit in a vendor’s cloud.

It doesn’t sit anywhere — there is no processing by us, no account needed for the core, and a connection log on the machine that proves the silence.
