PRIVACY & SECURITY

Private AI, with clear boundaries.

Core recording, transcription and summarization are designed to run locally. Optional integrations are separate and require your attention.

⌂

Core AI processing stays local

Audio transcription, summaries and meeting search use on-device models after initial download. Internet is required to download the application and model files.

✓

You approve what goes out

Follow-ups and documents are prepared for review. Your approval is required before sending or changing user files.

▤

Retention, redaction and backups

LocalNote includes reported consent logs, export redaction and local backups. Do not assume all files are encrypted at rest until that is separately verified.

↗

Connected clients need separate trust

An approved MCP client can read selected meeting data. That client's hosted AI services may transmit or store content even though LocalNote's own core is local.

What “offline” means here

After initial setup, core capture and local AI tasks can operate with internet disconnected. This does not mean software downloads, online calls, email sending or optional cloud-connected AI assistants work without internet.

Before public launch, publish an independently reproducible network audit and full storage/encryption details. Marketing claims should match the shipped release.

And because the app keeps a log of every connection it makes, this posture is something you can prove — to a client, an auditor, or anyone who asks where the data went — not just promise.
