Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Proofaro is built so your meetings stay yours. This page covers two things: the Proofaro app, and this website — including invite requests.

The app: your meetings stay on your device

  • Storage. Recordings, transcripts, summaries, notes and documents you create are stored on your own computer. Proofaro operates no server that receives your meeting data — there is nothing to upload to.
  • Processing. Transcription and the AI features run on your device, using models downloaded once during setup.
  • Recording consent. Before recording, the app shows a consent reminder. Each recording's consent is kept in an encrypted, tamper-evident log on your device.
  • Retention. Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. We hold no copy.
  • Deletion. You can delete any meeting, recording or document inside the app at any time. Because nothing reaches us, there is nothing on our side to delete.
  • Optional sharing. Files leave your device only when you export or send them yourself. The optional local MCP server is off by default; if you switch it on, you approve each connecting app individually, and every read is logged. If an app you approve uses an online AI, what it reads goes to that AI — the app warns you about exactly this before you enable it.
  • Usage counts. Optional and off by default. If you opt in, the app sends anonymous counts only — never meeting content.

This website and invite requests

  • What we collect. Your email address, the operating system and the role you choose in the form, and anything you choose to write in the form’s optional boxes. Nothing else.
  • This website. The site counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool — no cookies, and no tracking across sites. The invite form is handled by FormSubmit, which delivers your request to our mailbox. The Proofaro app itself contains no tracking.
  • What we send you. One acknowledgement email confirming your request — and, if your request is approved, your invitation. That is all. No newsletters and no marketing.
  • Retention. Requests are kept in that mailbox while invite-only early access is running, so approvals can be worked through in order. Ask us to delete yours at any time at support@localnote.ai and we will.
  • Never sold or shared. We do not sell email addresses, and we do not share them with advertisers.
  • This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of California.
