Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Proofaro is built so your meetings stay yours. This page covers two things: the Proofaro app, and this website — including invite requests.

The app: your meetings stay on your device

  • Storage. Recordings, transcripts, summaries, notes and documents you create are stored on your own computer. Proofaro operates no server that receives your meeting data — there is nothing to upload to.
  • Processing. Transcription and the AI features run on your device, using models downloaded once during setup.
  • Recording consent. The first time you record, the app asks you to confirm once that you will make sure everyone recorded knows and agrees, and it shows a consent reminder each time recording starts. Every recording, and the consent noted for it, is kept in a tamper-evident log on your device, encrypted whenever your system keychain is available.
  • Retention. Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. We hold no copy.
  • Deletion. You can delete any meeting, recording or document inside the app at any time. Because nothing reaches us, there is nothing on our side to delete.
  • Optional sharing. Files leave your device only when you export or send them yourself. The optional local MCP server is off by default; if you switch it on, you approve each connecting app individually, and every read is logged. If an app you approve uses an online AI, what it reads goes to that AI — the app warns you about exactly this before you enable it.
  • Usage counts. Optional and off by default. If you opt in, the app sends anonymous counts only to GoatCounter — counts of features used, never meeting content.

Send-time check (Outlook and Gmail)

Proofaro has an optional send-time check: an add-in for Outlook and an extension for Gmail in Chrome. It is off by default. It works only after you turn it on in the app’s Settings and pair the add-in or extension with a one-time token shown in the app. When it is on, the draft you are about to send — its text, recipients and subject — is passed to the Proofaro app on the same computer over a local (loopback) connection and checked against your meetings there. It is not sent to any server — Proofaro operates none — and it is not stored or sent anywhere else. If the app isn’t running, your email still sends, and you see a short line saying it wasn’t checked. If you never paired the add-in or extension, you see a line saying Proofaro isn’t connected yet.

This website and invite requests

  • What we collect. Your email address, the operating system and the role you choose in the form, and anything you choose to write in the form’s optional boxes. Nothing else.
  • This website. The site counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool — no cookies, and no tracking across sites. The invite form is handled by FormSubmit, which delivers your request to our mailbox. The Proofaro app itself contains no tracking.
  • What we send you. One acknowledgement email confirming your request — and, if your request is approved, your invitation. After that, occasional product updates about Proofaro — unsubscribe any time.
  • Retention. Requests are kept in that mailbox while invite-only early access is running, so approvals can be worked through in order. Ask us to delete yours at any time at support@proofaro.ai and we will.
  • Never sold or shared. We do not sell email addresses, and we do not share them with advertisers.
  • This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of California.

The free checker

The free checker at proofaro.ai/check runs entirely in your browser: the transcript and draft text you paste are checked on your own device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. We count anonymous events with GoatCounter — for example, that a check ran, or that an amount mismatch was found — never the text you pasted or the values in it. As with any visit to this website, GoatCounter also sees standard visit data: your browser, the page you came from (the referrer), your screen size and your country.

Your choices and rights

  • Your meeting data. It lives on your own computer. You can view, export and delete it in the app at any time.
  • Your email address. For the address you gave us in an invite request, write to support@proofaro.ai and we will correct or delete it. Wherever you live, you can also ask us what personal information we hold about you — in practice, that email address and what you chose to write in the form — and ask us to delete it.
  • Do Not Track. This website and the app do not track you across other sites and set no advertising cookies, so there is no tracking for a Do Not Track signal to switch off. GoatCounter counts visits without cookies.
  • Age. Proofaro is not for anyone under 16.
