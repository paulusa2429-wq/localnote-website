An Otter alternative for people who need offline — and proof

Otter is one of the best-known AI notetakers, and its cloud is genuinely good at turning meetings into searchable knowledge. Proofaro is built for the needs Otter's cloud cannot serve: transcription that runs on your own computer, works fully offline after a one-time setup download, keeps the recording with you, and checks later documents against what was actually said.

Meet Otter

Otter calls itself a meeting agent and notetaker: its OtterPilot joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls to record, transcribe and summarise, and Otter AI Chat then answers questions across your meetings — Otter describes it as being like ChatGPT for your meeting notes. It is a strong fit for teams who live inside their tools: notes sync to Salesforce and HubSpot, updates flow to Slack, and there are apps for the web, desktop, iPhone and Android, plus a Chrome extension. A free Basic plan (300 transcription minutes per user a month) makes it easy to start, and paid plans lift the limits.

What Otter does not try to be is private or offline. In Otter's own words, "Otter.ai is entirely cloud-based": your audio is uploaded to Otter's servers, and that is where transcription and summaries happen. Everything on this page follows from that one design choice.

When Proofaro is the better fit

You work offline

Flights, secure facilities, client sites with no guest Wi-Fi worth trusting — your meetings do not stop for a connection, and neither does the transcription. Proofaro runs on your computer, fully offline after a one-time setup download, so the plane lands and your notes are already done. Nothing waits on an upload; nothing sits in a queue hoping the network comes back. See offline meeting notes.

You need the receipts

The recording and the exact words stay on your machine, so “who agreed what” stops being an argument and becomes a lookup: find the moment, read the quote, play it back. And if a disagreement ever turns serious, you can hand over a self-verifying evidence pack — an export the other side can check for tampering, built from your own record, not from memory.

Mistakes cost you money

In the meeting, the renewal was agreed at $4,000 with onboarding included. The quote that arrives says $3,200 — with onboarding billed separately. (An example, but a pattern anyone in sales or procurement will recognise.) Bring Proofaro the quote, contract or follow-up, and the check runs as soon as you bring it: changed numbers, dates and promises are flagged against the original words, and a response draft is prepared for your review — never sent on its own. See Said vs Written and commitment tracking.

Your mornings start ahead

This is the habit that sticks. You sit down with your coffee and Proofaro has already done the homework: today’s brief is ready, yesterday’s documents have been checked against yesterday’s words, and the follow-up drafts are waiting in your Morning Queue for a tap. Your work is ready before you are — you start the day approving, not digging.

Your meetings mix languages

Plenty of real meetings switch mid-sentence — English with Hindi, English with Spanish. Proofaro locks the transcription language for the whole meeting, so the record stays clean instead of flipping between languages line by line, and your summaries can come back in English, Spanish or Hindi. The meeting sounds like your team actually talks, and the notes still read clearly.

A bot in the meeting changes the meeting

The moment a recording bot joins, people talk differently — clients go careful, candidates go quiet. No bot ever joins a Proofaro meeting. People talk freely, and the recordings and transcripts stay on your computer, never uploaded for processing. For client-confidential work, that is not a nice-to-have; it is the whole point.

Proofaro doesn’t just remember what happened. It helps catch what could go wrong next — and that is why, once it has caught one real mistake for you, checking it becomes part of the day.

Questions people ask

Can Otter transcribe without internet?

Not live. Otter's own guide says you can keep recording while offline, but the recording uploads when a stable connection returns and is transcribed on Otter's servers. Proofaro transcribes on your computer, so a flight or a dead site network changes nothing.

Does a bot have to join my meetings with Otter?

Only if you use OtterPilot, which joins as a participant. Otter's desktop app for Mac and Windows can record without the Notetaker joining — but the audio still goes to Otter's cloud for transcription. With Proofaro no bot ever joins, and nothing is uploaded for processing.

What does Otter cost, and what does Proofaro cost?

Otter's Basic plan is free with 300 transcription minutes per user a month; Pro is $8.33 per user a month billed annually, and Business is $19.99. Proofaro is free during early access while invites roll out in small waves; paid pricing has not been announced.

Can Otter check a quote against what was agreed?

Otter does not describe a built-in document check. Its blog shows asking Claude, connected to Otter, to compare past calls with written records — useful, but it is a chat answer. Proofaro's Said vs Written compares the document itself against the original words and shows the quote as evidence, on your machine.
