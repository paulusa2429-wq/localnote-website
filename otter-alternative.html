An Otter alternative for people who need offline — and proof

Otter is one of the best-known AI notetakers, and its cloud is genuinely good at turning meetings into searchable knowledge. Proofaro is built for the needs Otter's cloud cannot serve: transcription that runs on your own computer, works fully offline after a one-time setup download, keeps the recording with you, and checks later documents against what was actually said.

Meet Otter

Otter calls itself a meeting agent and notetaker: its OtterPilot joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls to record, transcribe and summarise, and Otter AI Chat then answers questions across your meetings — Otter describes it as being like ChatGPT for your meeting notes. It is a strong fit for teams who live inside their tools: notes sync to Salesforce and HubSpot, updates flow to Slack, and there are apps for the web, desktop, iPhone and Android, plus a Chrome extension. A free Basic plan (300 transcription minutes per user a month) makes it easy to start, and paid plans lift the limits.

What Otter does not try to be is private or offline. In Otter's own words, "Otter.ai is entirely cloud-based": your audio is uploaded to Otter's servers, and that is where transcription and summaries happen. Everything on this page follows from that one design choice.

Questions people ask

Can Otter transcribe without internet?

Not live. Otter's own guide says you can keep recording while offline, but the recording uploads when a stable connection returns and is transcribed on Otter's servers. Proofaro transcribes on your computer, so a flight or a dead site network changes nothing.

Does a bot have to join my meetings with Otter?

Only if you use OtterPilot, which joins as a participant. Otter's desktop app for Mac and Windows can record without the Notetaker joining — but the audio still goes to Otter's cloud for transcription. With Proofaro no bot ever joins, and nothing is uploaded for processing.

What does Otter cost, and what does Proofaro cost?

Otter's Basic plan is free with 300 transcription minutes per user a month; Pro is $8.33 per user a month billed annually, and Business is $19.99. Proofaro is free during early access while invites roll out in small waves; paid pricing has not been announced.

Can Otter check a quote against what was agreed?

Otter does not describe a built-in document check. Its blog shows asking Claude, connected to Otter, to compare past calls with written records — useful, but it is a chat answer. Proofaro's Said vs Written compares the document itself against the original words and shows the quote as evidence, on your machine.

When Proofaro is the better fit

Your audio shouldn’t have to live in a cloud

In Otter’s own words, it is “entirely cloud-based”: your audio uploads to Otter’s servers, and that is where transcription and summaries happen. Proofaro takes the opposite design decision — the recording, transcript and summary are made and kept on your computer, never uploaded for processing, and you can delete any of them at any time.

You work offline

Otter can keep recording while you are offline, but the audio uploads when you reconnect and is transcribed then — live transcription requires a connection. Proofaro transcribes on the machine in front of you, fully offline after a one-time setup download: on a plane, at a client site, anywhere the network is not invited.

A participant you didn’t invite

OtterPilot joins Zoom, Meet and Teams calls as a participant — and people talk differently when a recording bot is in the room: clients go careful, candidates go quiet. Otter’s desktop app can record bot-free, but the audio still leaves for the cloud. With Proofaro no bot ever joins, on any platform, because capture happens on your computer.

You need the receipts

Otter keeps your recording in its cloud, with synced playback. Proofaro keeps it on your computer — find the moment, read the exact quote, play it back. And if a disagreement ever turns serious, you can hand over a self-verifying evidence pack: an export whose fingerprints let the other side confirm nothing was altered after export, built from your record rather than your memory of it.

Asking a chatbot is not a document check

The renewal is agreed at $4,000 with onboarding included; the quote arrives at $4,800, onboarding billed separately. Otter describes no built-in document check — its blog shows asking a connected chatbot to compare calls with written records, which gives you an answer you have to take on trust. Proofaro’s Said vs Written compares the document itself against the recorded words, on your computer, and flags each change with the original quote as evidence — a response draft prepared for your review. Otter answers when you ask. Proofaro is ready before.

With Otter, the morning starts with a question typed into AI Chat and a search through a cloud workspace. Proofaro starts before you ask: your brief for today is already written, the documents that arrived yesterday have been run against yesterday’s recording, and drafts of the follow-ups sit in your Morning Queue waiting for one tap. You start the day approving — not searching for what was said.

Otter built a cloud that knows your meetings. Proofaro keeps that knowledge on your computer — and puts it to work checking the documents that follow.

Switching from Otter to Proofaro

What carries over. Your Otter recordings live in Otter’s cloud, under your workspace’s retention settings — so before you leave, download the meetings you want to keep. Audio you bring to Proofaro can be imported and turned into notes on your computer, and from then on the record lives with you, not in a workspace.

What your first week looks like. Request an invite, install Proofaro on your Mac, Windows or Linux computer, and finish the one-time setup download. Your next Zoom, Meet or Teams call is captured on that computer — no OtterPilot-style participant in the list, nothing uploaded afterwards — and quotes and follow-ups can be checked against the words with Said vs Written.

The honest part. Proofaro deliberately does not sync to Salesforce, HubSpot or Slack — if your team’s workflow runs on Otter’s integrations, that is a real reason to stay. Switch when the record itself matters more than the plumbing: when the meeting cannot leave the building, or the quote has to match the call.
