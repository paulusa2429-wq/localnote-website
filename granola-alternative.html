When Granola is the better fit

If you mainly want a lightweight notepad that tidies up your own typed notes, you are always online, and you do not need to keep recordings or check documents afterwards, Granola does that job well and simply.

When Proofaro is the better fit

  • You work offline — flights, secure facilities, client sites — and transcription still has to happen. See offline meeting notes.
  • You need the receipts — the recording and the exact words kept on your machine, so "who agreed what" is a lookup, not an argument.
  • Mistakes cost you money — quotes, contracts and follow-ups get checked against the meeting record automatically. See Said vs Written and commitment tracking.
