Compare Proofaro, honestly

Every meeting tool on this page is good at something, and each comparison below says where the other tool wins as plainly as where Proofaro does. What separates them is five questions — and one design choice: whether your meetings are processed in someone else's cloud, or on your own computer.

The five questions that decide it

  • Does a bot join? A named participant changes how people talk — and some tools send one by default.
  • Where does transcription happen? In a cloud you rent, or on the computer you own?
  • Does it work offline? Not "records now, transcribes later" — actually finished, with no connection at all.
  • Where does the recording live, and for how long? Kept, deleted, or kept-for-three-months are very different answers.
  • Can it check the paperwork? When the quote contradicts the call, which tool can prove it — with the original words?

Proofaro's answers: no bot ever joins; transcription runs on your computer; it works fully offline after a one-time setup download; the recording stays on your machine for as long as you keep it; and Said vs Written checks documents against the original words, with the quote as evidence. Here is how the others answer.

The comparisons

Proofaro vs Granola

Granola is bot-free like Proofaro — the closest cousin here. But its transcription is cloud-only, the recording is not kept, and there is no offline mode and no document check. Granola wins on availability today: Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android, open to anyone.

Proofaro vs Otter

Otter is the knowledge engine: OtterPilot joins your calls (or record bot-free on desktop), Otter AI Chat searches everything, and notes sync to Salesforce and HubSpot. It is, in its own words, entirely cloud-based — nothing runs offline or on your device.

Read the full Otter comparison →

Proofaro vs Fireflies

Fireflies sends Fred, a named bot, into your meetings, then pushes notes into 240+ apps with conversation intelligence on top. Powerful for sales teams — and entirely in Fireflies' US cloud, where your audio and video live.

Read the full Fireflies comparison →

Proofaro vs Fathom

Fathom is the generous one: unlimited free recordings, slick clips, a bot-free beta on Mac. It also states plainly that offline recording is not available, stores everything indefinitely in its US cloud, and has no Android app.

Read the full Fathom comparison →

Proofaro vs tl;dv

tl;dv is the sales team's favourite: clips, coaching scorecards, CRM fields that fill themselves, and EU hosting it deserves credit for. Recordings still live in its cloud — free-plan ones for 3 months — and nothing transcribes on your device.

Where Proofaro loses — for now

Honesty cuts both ways. Every tool above can be signed up for today; Proofaro is invite-only early access. They have mobile apps, team workspaces and hundreds of integrations; Proofaro has none of those yet, runs on Mac today with Windows and Linux in progress, and is built for one person's record, kept privately. If you need those things now, pick the tool that has them — and if you need offline and proof, that is what the invite is for.
