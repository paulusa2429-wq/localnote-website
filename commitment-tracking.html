Commitment tracking

Every promise from every meeting, tracked

Meetings run on promises: "I'll send the revised quote Friday." "Onboarding is included." "We'll confirm the date next week." Then the week moves on and nobody can prove what was agreed. Proofaro keeps a ledger of those commitments — who promised what, by when — drawn from the meeting record itself.

Promises become a ledger

As meetings are transcribed on your computer, Proofaro collects the commitments in them into one list: the promise, the person who made it, the date it was due, and the exact words behind it. No manual logging, no separate tracker to keep up to date — the ledger is built from what was actually said.

Nothing quietly slips

  • Due dates surface in your day. Your morning queue shows what is due, what is overdue, and what changed since yesterday.
  • Changes get flagged. If a later meeting or document moves a deadline, drops a promise or changes a number, the ledger entry is checked against it — the same machinery behind Said vs Written.
  • Answers come with receipts. Ask "what did we agree with the client last month?" and Proofaro answers from your meetings with the original quote attached, so you can check it yourself.

Tracked locally, like everything else

The ledger lives on your computer with the rest of your meeting record. Proofaro works fully offline after a one-time setup download, and no server ever receives your meetings.
