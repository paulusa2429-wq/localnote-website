Questions people ask

How does the free checker work?

Paste the transcript of a meeting and the draft you are about to send — an email, proposal or statement of work. The checker pulls the amounts, dates, names and promises out of both texts, lines each draft sentence up against the meeting record, and flags every place where the two disagree, with the exact quote from the meeting beside it.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The check runs entirely in your browser, on this page. Your transcript and draft are never sent, stored or logged — and you can prove it yourself: open your browser's Network tab and run a check; no request carries your text. The page only counts anonymous usage events, such as how many checks were run, never the content.

What does it catch?

Changed amounts (including spoken numbers like "eight thousand"), moved dates and deadlines, swapped names and owners, changed quantities, and promises in your draft that were never made in the meeting — those promise flags are marked Beta. It does not judge tone, and a rewording that keeps the same meaning is not flagged.

How is this different from the Proofaro app?

This page checks text you paste. The Proofaro app records your meetings itself, runs the same kind of check automatically against your live meeting record, and works fully offline after setup — no pasting, and the original recording stays on your computer as proof.
