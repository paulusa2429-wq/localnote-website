Security

Last updated 19 November 2026

Everything here describes the shipped app, stated exactly as it is — including the parts that are not encrypted.

Where the data goes — and doesn't

  • A meeting is recorded by the app on your computer. Transcription and all AI processing run on that computer. Transcripts, summaries, ledgers and documents are stored on that computer.
  • Proofaro operates no server that receives customer meeting data. Nothing is uploaded for processing, because there is nothing to upload to.
  • Files leave the machine only when you export or send them.

Models and network behavior

  • Speech and language models are open-weight models downloaded once during setup (about 4.8 GB in total) from their public repositories, then used entirely on-device. Additional models download only when you choose them.
  • After setup, core work needs no internet at all — it runs in airplane mode. The app connects to the network for update checks (automatic; you can turn them off in Settings), for downloads you start (setup and models), and, if you opt in, anonymous usage counts — off by default.

Encryption, stated exactly

  • The consent log is encrypted and hash-chained, so entries cannot be quietly altered.
  • Recordings, transcripts and the app's database are not separately encrypted by Proofaro. Protect them with your operating system's full-disk encryption — FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows — the same control you would apply to any sensitive local file. We state this plainly because a security review should never have to discover it.
  • Evidence packages you export carry a SHA-256 fingerprint of every file, so whoever receives one can check that nothing changed after export. The fingerprints are not proof that the contents are true, the package itself is not encrypted, and an export covers a whole meeting — protect the file like the meeting it contains.

Your device is the boundary

  • Proofaro's protection assumes your computer account is yours. Anyone who can unlock your device and open your account can open Proofaro, like any other app — on a shared computer, use separate accounts, and lock your screen when you step away.
  • Backups copy everything on your disk, including Proofaro's data. If you back up your computer, encrypt the backup too — for example, an encrypted Time Machine backup.
  • Full-disk encryption differs by system: FileVault on macOS, BitLocker or device encryption on Windows, LUKS on Linux. Turning it on is the single strongest step you can take.

Consent: a reminder is not consent

The app shows a consent reminder before recording and keeps a log of each recording's consent — but the reminder does not obtain consent for you. Getting consent from the people you record is your responsibility, as set out in the Terms.

Connected tools, on a short leash

  • The local MCP server is off by default. When you switch it on, each AI client is approved individually, limited to the meetings you select, and every access is written to an audit log.
  • Local is not the same as offline: a connected client is a deliberate, logged exception you grant — never a background behavior. If that client uses an online AI, what it reads goes to that AI.

Licensing

  • License keys are cryptographically signed and verified on your device. There is no server check and no location or IP tracking in the app.
